CODE OF CONDUCT - Tenggara Precision Sdn. Bhd. - SYNTHETIC SAMPLE
Version 2.0, approved by the Board on 14 February 2025; this is the version in force at the Klang plant throughout FY2025.
The code of conduct requires lawful, honest conduct, respectful treatment of workers, protection of customer information, and accurate environmental and safety records.
The anti-corruption commitment prohibits bribes, kickbacks, and facilitation payments; the detailed Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy is version 4.0, approved on 14 February 2025 (A-05).
The whistleblowing commitment provides a confidential and anonymous reporting channel, bans retaliation, and refers to the Whistleblowing Policy in A-05, version 4.0 approved on 14 February 2025.
Policy owner: Compliance Manager. Applies to employees, directors, and contractors at the Klang plant; separate group entities maintain their own policies.
Policy commitments in force: Code of Conduct version 2.0, Anti-Corruption Policy version 4.0, and Whistleblowing Policy version 4.0; all approved 14 February 2025.
